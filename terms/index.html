Terms of Use

Last updated: November 1, 2026

These terms are the rules for using the Grip Raven app. Grip Raven is run by Aaron Udho, a sole proprietor in Ontario, Canada. Contact: support@gripraven.com

1. What the app is

A tool to log food, workouts, weight and water, set reminders, and get general fitness information. This includes an optional AI coach and a food label scan.

2. Who can use it

You must be 16 or older. The app is offered in Canada, outside Quebec. Please do not use it if you live in Quebec.

Pre-workout ratings are for 18 and over. The Community tab, ratings and product requests are only for people 18 or older. The app uses the birthday you type in Profile to hide them, and we cannot check it. If you are under 18, do not use them.

3. Not medical advice

Everything in the app about fitness, food, calories, protein, water, reminders, creatine, pre-workouts and training programs is general information. It is not medical, dietitian or coaching advice.

Talk to a doctor, physiotherapist or dietitian before you change your diet or training, or if you have pain, an injury or a medical condition. If you feel very unwell, call 911 or go to an emergency room.

4. Use at your own risk

Exercise has risks. You are responsible for how you train, what you eat and drink, and for checking food labels.

Food data from Open Food Facts and USDA, numbers read from a photo, and numbers the app works out (calories, protein, body fat, estimated max, levels) are estimates. They can be wrong. Training programs are examples, not a plan made for you. Never test a true max alone or without a spotter.

5. AI coach and label scan

AI features are optional. They need an account and your separate yes. They may not be available yet. See the Privacy Policy.

AI answers and numbers are made by a computer. They can be wrong or out of date. Check each number against the label. Do not rely on them for medical decisions. Do not type your name, contact details or other people's information into the coach.

6. Your account and data

Keep your login safe. You own your data. You give us permission to store and process it to run the app. Your profile, weight, water, reminder and badge information stays on your phone and is not uploaded.

You can export your data and delete your account in Settings. Deleting your account deletes your cloud data. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

7. Acceptable use

Do not abuse the AI, try to break or overload the app, or use it for anything illegal. We may limit AI use per person per day, and we may switch AI features off at any time.

8. Paid plans

Paid plans are not offered yet. If they are added, Apple will handle payment under Apple's own terms and the price will be shown before you pay.

9. Third-party data and services

Food data: © Open Food Facts contributors, used under the Open Database License. See the Open Food Facts legal page.

Food guide numbers come from USDA FoodData Central (fdc.nal.usda.gov).

AI answers come from Anthropic's models. Login and storage come from Supabase. Each company handles data under its own policy. See the Privacy Policy for the links.

The Pre-workouts section is just Aaron's opinion, to help you choose. It is not sponsored and it is not an ad. It is not a safety or quality rating and it is not medical advice. Product names belong to their owners. The app is not connected to them.

10. Community pre-workout ratings

What a rating is. A rating is a star score (1 to 5) and up to 5 preset tags, saved with your account. It is one person's opinion. It is not a safety or quality rating and not medical advice. The product list is chosen by Aaron. Other users see only totals, averages and common tags, not who rated. Aaron can see which account rated and uses that only to run the service, handle reports and stop abuse.

Be honest. Rate only products you have used. Do not rate a product you make or sell, or that you are paid or given anything free to promote. Do not use more than one account, ask others to rate for you, or give or accept rewards for ratings. Do not try to push a product's score up or down.

Requests and reports. You may ask us to add a product. Do not type names that are rude, hateful, misleading or contain personal details, or links to anything other than the product page. We check every request and may refuse it. You can report a listing as wrong. We aim to look at reports within 3 days.

Our right to remove. We may remove or change any rating, request or listing, and suspend or end accounts that break these rules or that we think are false or abusive.

Brand owners and anyone who objects. Email support@gripraven.com with the product, what is wrong, and who you are. We aim to reply within 3 days. We fix listings that are wrong (such as a wrong name). We do not promise to remove honest opinions.

Permission. You let us store your rating and show it in totals and averages inside the app. You keep the rest of your rights. Remove a rating, or delete your account, to take it out.

Names. Brand and product names are shown only to identify products. Grip Raven is not connected to those brands.

11. Changes and ending

We may change the app or these terms. The date at the top shows the latest version. We may suspend accounts that break these rules, or that we learn belong to someone who cannot use the app, for example someone under 16 or in Quebec, or someone who breaks the rating rules above.
